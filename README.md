# SentIta – Italian Sentiment Lexicon

**SentIta** is a sentiment lexicon specifically developed for the **Italian language** by **Serena Pelosi**, computational linguist and researcher.

Originally developed in 2014 as part of her doctoral research and presented at CLiC-it 2015, SentIta is a lexical resource designed to support lexicon-based sentiment analysis of Italian-language texts.

The project has evolved from a lexical database into **Sentita Dashboard**, an online linguistic analysis toolkit for Italian texts.

## 🆕 SentIta is now available as an online tool!

The original SentIta lexicon and its rule-based methodology have evolved into **Sentita Dashboard**, a web-based toolkit for the linguistic analysis of Italian texts.

## Available Versions

### 1. SentIta Lite – Lexical Resource (GitHub)

This repository provides a reduced version of the original SentIta lexicon, containing approximately **1,000 Italian lexical entries**.

The resource includes linguistic and sentiment-related annotations designed for research, experimentation and the development of lexicon-based sentiment analysis applications for the Italian language.

### 2. Sentita Lite – Online Tool (Free Access Upon Request)

Sentita Lite provides an online environment for analyzing Italian-language texts through lexical resources and explicit linguistic rules.

The Sentita toolkit extends the original methodology through complementary analytical modules, including:

- **Sentiment Analysis:** identification of positive and negative orientations, sentiment intensity and contextual modifiers.
- **Emotion Detection:** exploration of eight emotional categories through a dedicated proprietary Italian emotion lexicon.
- **Topic Analysis:** identification and exploration of user-defined topics using transparent, configurable lexical criteria.
- **Data Exploration and Visualization:** inspection of lexical occurrences, distributions, comparisons and graphical representations.

The Lite edition provides access to a reduced set of resources and functionalities compared with the complete system.

The methodology is designed to be interpretable and verifiable, allowing researchers to examine the linguistic evidence contributing to the results.

## Lexicon Structure and Methodology

SentIta assigns linguistic and sentiment-related information to each Italian lemma, including:

- **Part of Speech (PoS):** grammatical category.
- **Polarity:** positive or negative sentiment orientation.
- **Intensity:** intense or weak sentiment expression.
- **Sentiment Score:** numerical value ranging from **−3 (strongly negative) to +3 (strongly positive)**.

The original lexicon was developed to interact with **Doxa**, a rule-based prototype for *Document-level Opinionated teXt Analysis*.

The approach combines lexical annotations with linguistic rules that account for contextual phenomena, including negation and intensification, to support the interpretation of sentiment in Italian-language texts.

This methodology follows an interpretable, **white-box approach** to sentiment analysis, allowing researchers to inspect the linguistic elements contributing to sentiment scores.

SentIta originated in Serena Pelosi's doctoral research and has subsequently been presented, applied, compared and discussed in several scientific publications.

### Independent Studies Citing or Using SentIta

SentIta has also been referenced, employed and compared in independent research addressing different aspects of Italian-language sentiment analysis.

The following publications are authored by researchers other than Serena Pelosi and demonstrate the use or examination of the lexical resource in different scientific contexts.

  Application and evaluation of SentIta in the analysis of Italian political communication and social media engagement.

### Additional Methodological Reference

The emotion detection component of Sentita uses a separate proprietary lexicon developed for Italian. Its intensity-based approach draws inspiration from research on graded word–emotion associations, including:

- Mohammad, S. M. (2018). [Word Affect Intensities](https://aclanthology.org/L18-1027/). *Proceedings of the Eleventh International Conference on Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC 2018)*.

The Italian emotion lexicon is distinct from the original SentIta sentiment resource and is not distributed through this repository.
